# Mission Reflection

Completing this cloud computing mission helped me understand the differences between the three major cloud platforms: **AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. The platform that impressed me the most was **Google Cloud** because of its strong features in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I also found GCP interesting because Google created Kubernetes and provides Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) for managing containerized applications.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide important cloud services such as **virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and identity management**. They also allow businesses to scale their resources depending on their needs. However, each platform has different strengths. AWS has a very wide range of services, Azure is strongly connected to Microsoft technologies, and GCP is known for AI, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. A company needs to consider its **budget, existing technology, security needs, location, performance, and future growth**. For example, a company that already uses Windows Server and Microsoft 365 may benefit more from Azure, while a company focused on AI and machine learning may prefer GCP.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about selecting the most popular provider. The best choice depends on the **specific needs and goals of the client**. I also learned how to compare cloud services and recommend solutions based on different business situations.

Finally, this mission helped me improve my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio**. I was able to organize my research, comparisons, recommendations, and Linux investigation in one place. It also gave me practical experience with cloud concepts and made me more confident in explaining cloud technologies and making basic cloud platform recommendations.
